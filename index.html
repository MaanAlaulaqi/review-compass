Read the scores carefully.

Model scores are uncalibrated. They are not verified probabilities of correctness. A low score does not create a separate “uncertain” class.

Neutral includes reviews associated with three-star ratings, which can contain mixed opinions. Matching some vocabulary features does not establish that the model understands the input language.

What improved?

DistilBERT corrected 90 baseline mistakes and introduced 57 errors on reviews the baseline got right. Epoch 1 was selected using validation macro-F1 (0.7995 versus 0.7985 at epoch 2). Neutral was the hardest class: test F1 0.7231.

What the result cannot establish

The interval describes uncertainty over sampled products for these fixed models. It does not measure variability over training seeds. Rating labels are noisy sentiment proxies, and the balanced All_Beauty sample does not represent all Amazon categories or natural rating prevalence.

Live browser inference uses Logistic Regression. DistilBERT scores above come from the completed Colab experiment; its saved weights are not loaded by this page.
